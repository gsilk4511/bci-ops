## How to configure it
1. Open Team, make a saved permission profile, and select each module's mode. In Read & write, enable only the ordinary and specialized actions needed. Deletion is separate. Work-order Trash is still protected by the administrator role and typed confirmation.
2. For record-scoped modules, choose All (existing access), Own (known creator or ownership evidence), or Assigned (explicit Team assignment). Use Assign records to staff. If project/client scopes are also restricted, assign related parents deliberately. Legacy records without reliable attribution can be assigned explicitly; do not guess their owner by name.
3. Preview a specific user's effective access. Save and test using separate staff sign-ins. Changing assignments or permissions refreshes open sessions on their next access check. Close active timers before revoking clock permissions; clock-out/cancel will also stop being available if the clock grant is removed.
4. A user with My Time and restricted work-order access still sees their own logged time, without a link to or title for a work order they cannot access. Existing self-service contact/leave and admin pay/balance controls remain in My Time & Profile when granted.
5. Restricted billing views use only the records visible to the user; the approval or adjustment of actual prior invoices/payroll remains an office process. For shared-stock operations, use an explicitly authorized role with all applicable inventory access.

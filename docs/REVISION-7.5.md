# BCI Operations revision .7.5
Build specification from the November 19 design discussions. Baseline: the Mobile Work Orders source package, not the identically versioned older package. Stage and test this release before .8.5; no deployment or live-data changes.

## Tickets and client operations
- Four queues: Work Order Tickets (submitted only; office review or correction with reason; return only after resubmission), Chasing Work (manual or genuinely new opportunities from email; source, notes, complete; no billing-client prerequisite), Projects (one row per open project, work-order progress and next step), Invoicing (closed projects with final billing or a blocker outstanding). Preserve unrelated actionable email, due dates, completed history and safe undo, and re-use existing linked tasks and permissions.
- Internal Client Portal: billing and contact details, primary contacts, projects, estimates and work-order links, and permissioned money rollup (received, outstanding, ready to bill).
- Project closes only via Close Project on the project screen. New work order reopens it. Preserve prior invoices and other history. Do not infer closed status from reviewed or billed orders.
- Project Billing: down payment at project level, recorded requested/received/applied amounts and traceable credits; progress invoice one or more reviewed unbilled orders without closing; final billing shows remaining eligible work, prior invoices, closeout blockers and credits. Never send automatically or duplicate billed work. Payment collection stays in Money.

## Estimate to work orders
- Guided estimate under an existing or newly created billing client and project. An estimate can have multiple scopes of each type. Copper asks building, access, drop count, cable type, compatible jack, length; calculate cable boxes, jacks, hardware, and labor. Fiber asks route, length, type, strands, termination, testing and labeling. Business-provided catalog and formula defaults remain adjustable, missing prices visible. Shared equipment such as scissor lift, delivery, and mobilization entered once. Calibrate inputs later against itemized supplier and QuickBooks costs plus comparable job field records; no invented calibrated rates.
- Internal cost and margin, separate printable/emailable client document and on-site signature; store exact approved revision and options. Changes after signature are separate revisions.
- Approval generates drafts once: inventory/parts shortages, optional lift, copper, fiber, and closeout with test results, drawings, photos, and email preparation. Link to approved revision. Office reviews and releases; no external sends, buys, rentals, invoicing, or closing automatically. Closeout gates final billing, not progress billing. Never rewrite released work silently.

## Staff and work orders
- Save profile name, contact email, pay scale, vacation and sick balances with sensitive administration separate from self-service. Time entries use authenticated employee identity; authorized corrections preserve employee and actor identities. My Time groups own work and leave by week/pay period with source links. Vacation/sick entry separate, with approval and balance tracking. Distinct internal tasks each receive a work order under Randy Bevins → BCI ELITE ADMIN; counts as work but cannot be customer invoiced.
- Submit Time: always-visible mandatory work notes, preserve entered time on validation failure. Submit Materials: Add Receipt camera or photo upload, attach to work order and optionally entry, no duplicate material charge. Time and parts row edit/remove applies only to own work for staff, any rows for authorized admins. Reconcile stock, costing, review and payroll; finalized records require adjustments rather than silent rewrites.
- Diagnose and fix admin-only Delete Work Order. Show dependencies and confirm with literal delete. Recoverable Trash for eligible work; reconcile links, inventory, tickets and totals; preserve audit and payroll evidence. Explicit blockers for invoices/finalized payroll.

## Release
Test combined copper/fiber, progress then final invoice, idempotent approval, reopening and nonduplication, work order return/resubmission, receipts, staff ownership, internal time, and reversible deletion. Preserve mobile layout, existing records, DB and environment settings. Deliver one clean Source ZIP for this revision, distinct version next to the BCI logo, no secrets or nested source.

## Implementation notes for staging
- Office triages new email into Chasing Work, with conversation linking and deduplication. General actionable email remains separate. The app does not guess a job or auto-create a billing client from an unknown sender.
- The estimate Email action opens a draft in the system mail app. Save the estimate to PDF and attach it manually. The stored on-device signature captures the approved scope, consent and snapshot; it does not verify the physical person's authority. Alternatives/optional selections can be described; fully automated alternate pricing is not included.
- My Time uses authenticated entry identity when present. Review legacy names and pay balances in staging. The internal shortcut needs one matching project under the named billing client. No live client or project is seeded automatically.
- Admin deletion is recoverable by default. The application blocks invoices, finalized payroll, active clocks and inconsistent stock history; it shows the reason instead of changing an accounting record. Adjustments for booked invoices/payroll remain an office process.

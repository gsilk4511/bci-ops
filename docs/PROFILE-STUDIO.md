# Profile Studio

Open My Time & Profile and edit your profile. Choose from 20 looks for each
of six categories: avatar ring, page lighting, banner, section color, text
color, and technician icon. Try mixes in the preview before saving. Reset
the appearance to use the default look, without changing the profile photo,
name, bio or social links. Styles use built-in options; no remote assets or
custom CSS are required.

Your saved appearance follows your internal profile. Coworkers can browse
profile looks and shared contact links, but not your payroll, time-off history,
private job metrics, or Business Manager records. Only individuals with the
existing self-profile edit grant can save their own appearance. Icons are
personal decoration, not certification, job title or access level.

Internal work-order icons are linked to employee accounts, not display names.
Older entries that are not linked to an account cannot be matched reliably.
The client print view, documents and exports do not use the new icons.

## Staging smoke test

1. Sign in as a staff test account. Try a different choice in every category,
   check the live preview, save, reload and confirm the selections persisted.
2. Check the same profile from a second staff test account. Verify the look
   is visible and personal/private information is not.
3. Use a synthetic work order with a time entry linked to the first employee.
   Verify the icon beside their entry on the internal work-order screen and
   confirm it does not appear in the client printout.
4. Check a narrow screen and reduced-motion mode. Reset the appearance and
   confirm the employee's other profile details stayed in place.

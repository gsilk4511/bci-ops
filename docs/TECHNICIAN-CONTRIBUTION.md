# What the Technician Contribution chart means

This is a private, project-based indicator, not a commission, payroll figure,
paid-cash report, profit figure, or promise of a bonus. No sales role is used.
It gives an employee a share of supported billed work-order revenue based on
verified labor hours on that work order. It aggregates shares by project so an
employee does not receive credit from unrelated work orders on the same job.

The model recognizes supported reviewed work with a verified grouped-invoice
snapshot on sent or paid, non-void invoices. It uses billed amounts before
sales tax and does not subtract applied deposits or credits from earned work.
It does not attribute work on a name match, travel-only entries, duplicate
billing references, internal orders, or unsupported legacy invoice formats.
Unlinked labor is not credited to a named staff member. Available results are
therefore sometimes partial. An unavailable result is not a zero valuation.

Profit and Employment cost are explicitly reserved; there is no calculation
of loaded labor costs, benefits, overhead, net profit, or bonus payouts.
Only the signed-in person's contribution is projected to their profile;
peer profile records never contain contribution, payroll or leave data.

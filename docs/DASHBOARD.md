# Dashboard
Dashboard is the main workspace. The office defaults mirror the original Home layout: work orders awaiting review, reviewed orders ready to invoice, invoices awaiting payment, open projects, and business overview. Field permissions show corresponding work/project cards. The old Tickets shortcut card is gone. The **Service desk** link remains for actual tickets, including existing opportunities, email workflows and old ticket links.

1. Open **Edit dashboard**. Pick a permitted card from the drop-down and choose **Add card**, or remove a card from the layout. Removing a card never deletes data.
2. On desktop/wide screens, drag a card by its top handle or resize from its bottom-right corner. Position and size snap to a grid, and overlapping cards shift down.
3. Use **Position & size** as an alternative on desktop or phone. On narrow screens, the app stacks cards in grid order and keeps the desktop grid layout intact.
4. Choose **Save layout** to save to your own login. **Cancel** discards your draft. **Reset layout** restores the defaults when saved. Existing roles and record visibility are unchanged.

Staging: Try dragging, resizing, adding, removing, and saving. Reload as the same account and sign in as a different account to verify layouts do not cross over. Narrow the viewport or check a phone. Follow a work order and a project link, and confirm a legacy review-ticket link still opens service desk actions. No migration is required.
